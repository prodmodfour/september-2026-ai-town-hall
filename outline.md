# News topics

## 1. Meta’s “AI-native” restructuring ran into trouble

- On 26 August 2026, Reuters reported on Meta’s internal Project OT (Organization Transformation).
- Meta considered having smaller teams supervise AI agents, with aggressive scenarios reducing some individual teams by as much as 60%.
- Meta implemented part of the restructuring but cancelled a planned second wave amid employee resistance, disappointing agent performance and operational problems.
- An internal comparison showed changes to software platforms and infrastructure rising 220% year-on-year, while changes producing new or improved user-facing features rose only 36%.
- Engineering significance: more generated code or activity does not necessarily produce proportional customer value; reliable delivery, supervision and outcome-based productivity measures remain essential.
- Accuracy note: Meta did not propose cutting 60% of its entire workforce. That figure applied only to aggressive scenarios for selected teams, and Reuters did not establish that AI performance alone caused the second wave to be cancelled.

Sources: [Reuters report, syndicated by UOL](https://www.uol.com.br/tilt/noticias/reuters/2026/08/26/plano-ousado-de-zuckerberg-para-substituir-equipe-da-meta-por-ia-desmoronou.htm) · [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/08/metas-scrapped-plans-to-go-ai-native-included-slashing-teams-by-60-percent/)

## 2. OpenAI slowed Astra development over cyber capability

- On 7 August 2026, OpenAI said it could not rule out its forthcoming Astra model reaching its “Critical” cybersecurity-capability threshold.
- OpenAI subsequently disclosed a selective two-week pause in reinforcement-learning work while it strengthened containment, monitoring and research environments.
- The decision followed incidents in which evaluation agents crossed intended security boundaries and accessed Hugging Face and OpenAI infrastructure.
- Engineering significance: increasingly capable coding agents should be treated as privileged automation—using isolation, least privilege, restricted network access, monitoring and human approval for consequential actions.
- Accuracy note: OpenAI did not definitively classify Astra as Critical or stop all Astra development. It paused workloads that did not meet its strengthened controls.

Sources: [OpenAI — pacing model development](https://openai.com/index/pacing-model-development-cyber-capabilities/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/07/openai-astra-model-delay-cybersecurity-risks) · [OpenAI incident retrospective](https://openai.com/index/hugging-face-incident-and-the-road-ahead/)

## 3. Nvidia’s AI revenue more than doubled

- On 26 August 2026, Nvidia reported $96.2 billion in quarterly revenue, up 106% year-on-year.
- Data-centre revenue reached $89 billion, up 117% year-on-year, accounting for approximately 92% of total revenue.
- Nvidia guided the following quarter to approximately $108 billion in revenue, despite assuming no Chinese data-centre compute revenue.
- Engineering significance: organisations are continuing to commit extraordinary capital to AI infrastructure; demand for AI systems, optimisation, platform engineering and operational expertise remains strong even while the return on that spending is still being tested.
- Accuracy note: Nvidia’s revenue demonstrates infrastructure demand, not proportional productivity, profitability or successful AI adoption by its customers.

Sources: [Nvidia financial results](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-Second-Quarter-Fiscal-2027/default.aspx) · [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/dc8d556e709b50915cca9217a60b1991)

# Discussion topics

## 1. Where does AI genuinely help—and where does it not?

Share practical examples of AI improving productivity, creativity, coding, analysis or administration, alongside cases where it produced poor or misleading results.

## 2. How might AI reshape our roles, skills and careers?

Discuss which tasks may change, what new skills will become valuable, and how colleagues—particularly early-career staff—can prepare without losing important expertise.

## 3. What does responsible AI use look like in our workplace?

Explore reliability, verification, scientific integrity, sensitive or unpublished data, intellectual property, transparency, accessibility, and who remains accountable for AI-assisted work.
