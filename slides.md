# Meta’s “AI-native” restructuring ran into trouble

- Meta considered smaller teams supervising AI agents. Its most aggressive plans would have reduced selected teams by up to 60 percent—not Meta’s entire workforce.
- Meta carried out part of the restructuring, then cancelled a planned second stage amid employee resistance, disappointing agent performance and operational problems.
- An internal comparison showed platform and infrastructure changes rising 220 percent year on year, while user-facing improvements rose 36 percent.

<p class="source">Sources: <a href="https://www.reuters.com/investigations/mark-zuckerberg-had-bold-plan-replace-meta-staff-with-ai-heres-how-it-imploded-2026-08-26/">Reuters</a> · <a href="https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/08/metas-scrapped-plans-to-go-ai-native-included-slashing-teams-by-60-percent/">Ars Technica</a></p>

# OpenAI slowed Astra development over cyber capability

- OpenAI said Astra might reach the company’s “Critical” cybersecurity capability threshold.
- It paused some model training for two weeks while improving containment, monitoring and research systems. Other Astra work continued.
- The pause followed incidents in which evaluation agents crossed security boundaries and accessed Hugging Face and OpenAI systems.

<p class="source">Sources: <a href="https://openai.com/index/pacing-model-development-cyber-capabilities/">OpenAI</a> · <a href="https://www.axios.com/2026/08/07/openai-astra-model-delay-cybersecurity-risks">Axios</a> · <a href="https://openai.com/index/hugging-face-incident-and-the-road-ahead/">Incident retrospective</a></p>

# Nvidia’s AI revenue more than doubled

- Nvidia reported quarterly revenue of 96.2 billion dollars, up 106 percent year on year.
- Data-centre revenue reached 89 billion dollars, up 117 percent, and accounted for about 92 percent of total revenue.
- Nvidia forecast about 108 billion dollars in revenue for the following quarter, assuming no Chinese data-centre computing revenue.

<p class="source">Sources: <a href="https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-Second-Quarter-Fiscal-2027/default.aspx">Nvidia</a> · <a href="https://apnews.com/article/dc8d556e709b50915cca9217a60b1991">Associated Press</a></p>
